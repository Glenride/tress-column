The fee that waits

Zero until ten million.
Then 0.3%, in the open.

Tress Column earns nothing from a launch until the launch itself has done serious volume — and even then, the take is small, fixed, disclosed, and never paid in the project’s own token. The schedule below is the Platinum tier; the full tier table and the locked deal that governs all three tiers follow.

0%

Below the gate

No platform fee at all under $10M in cumulative volume. The swap that crosses the gate still pays nothing — the fee applies only to later trades.

$10M

The gate, printed up front

The threshold is converted to quote-asset units once, at launch, and printed on the certificate. Anyone can re-derive it from the launch block. No price feed sits in the trade path.

0.3%

Fixed, and it can never move

Above the gate the fee is the launch’s configured rate — 30 basis points for a Platinum launch — fixed at inception and never raised, for that launch, ever.

0.0194 ETH

Airdrops, one flat fee

Merkle airdrop campaigns cost one exact, flat fee per campaign, with overpayment rejected. Claims are trustless against a frozen list. Optional — never required to launch.
