The exchange & the back office

Ops Console

Designed — not built

Staff-facing · the SaaS back office

The working side of Tress Column: memberships, campaigns, monitoring, and the public record — run from one console that is deliberately powerless over the contracts.

  • Memberships & campaigns. Plus+ subscriptions, airdrop campaigns, and launch monitoring in one place.
  • Certificates & attestations. Certificate issuance, plus Glenride attestations anchored on-chain as timestamp proofs — hashes only, no content.
  • Fees accounted from receipts. Treasury-fee accounting reads on-chain receipts — there is no internal ledger to disagree with the chain.
  • Powerless by design. The console holds no customer balances, approves no withdrawals, and freezes no one. A fault here can mislead — it cannot move a launch's locked liquidity.

Both surfaces are fast-follow work: they only have customers once launches exist, so the contract core ships first.
