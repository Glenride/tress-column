The certificate is the proof

Every launch earns
its certificate.

The certificate is not a badge someone grants you. It is the record the contracts themselves keep — the whole launch, printed plain, readable by anyone, forever.

One certificate is a launch. Many certificates, strung together, are a strand: launches that all played by the same visible rules, discoverable together, checkable together.

The design shown here is the certificate format from the product spec. Its final rendering is still being signed off — and the first real certificate, Tress Column #0, has not been minted yet.
